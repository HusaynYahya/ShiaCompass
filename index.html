How the placements were made

The three spectrums come from Zoheir Esmail's post, which draws on Shaykh Dr Vinay Khetia's framing. The scholars' positions are approximate readings of what each is best known for in their published work and in the secondary literature; the post itself places no one. Every placement is contestable, since most scholars hold mixed positions and changed over a lifetime. A position says nothing about piety or correctness.

Your answers are stored only in your own browser on this device (its local storage), so the test resumes where you left off and your result is there when you come back. They are never sent to a server. A result code lets you carry your answers to another device yourself. "Clear my data" on the insights page removes everything.

The test is a self-reflection tool, not a validated measure. The reading, activity and belief suggestions on the insights page describe tendencies among people with similar views, not predictions about you.
