# Scholar portraits for the share card

The result card shows a portrait for each scholar listed. Add an image here named after the scholar's id, as a JPEG, for example `sistani.jpg`. A square image, about 300 × 300 pixels, works best. Only add images you have the right to use, such as your own photos or ones with a licence that allows reuse.

Any scholar without an image gets a tile with their initials in their region's colour.
